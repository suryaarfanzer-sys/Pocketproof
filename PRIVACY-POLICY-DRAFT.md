# PocketProof Privacy Policy — Draft

**Status: Draft template. Do not publish until the publisher verifies and adapts it to the actual app, hosting, analytics, billing, and jurisdiction.**

PocketProof is intended to help users record personal items and warranty information.

## Data handling in this candidate
- Item names, brand, category, serial number, purchase date, warranty date, price, notes, and optional image are stored in the browser's local storage on the user's device.
- This candidate does not send those records to a PocketProof server.
- Users can export a JSON backup. Anyone who obtains that backup may be able to read its contents.
- Clearing browser/site data can erase saved records.
- Browser notification permission, if granted, is used for reminder checks while the app is opened. This candidate does not guarantee background reminders.
